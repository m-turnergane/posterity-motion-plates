## Secondhand vs torture (gate point)
Two different evidence pools, keep them separate in VO and storyboard:
- The SERVANTS' confessions at Bytča were obtained under torture (S3 "kínvallatás"; Thurzó's group). L08 covers this.
- The 1610 to 1611 INQUEST WITNESSES (about three hundred, nobles, clergy, townsfolk) were questioned by commission, not tortured; much of that was hearsay (L14). Never show witnesses being tortured, and never imply the hearsay came from torture.

## Visual reconstruction checks (for Scriptor and Atelier)
- Arrest: stage at the MANOR house in the village below the castle, at a supper table, winter, candles, armed men in a doorway. Not in the hilltop great hall. Thurzó's letter also reports finding a dead girl and an injured one at the manor; that is his (interested) claim and is OUT under the no-gore rule. Do not show.
- Transfer: her being taken up the hill to the castle at night is supported (letter, Craft). The servants' transfer to Bytča is supported; Bytča is a Renaissance manor/castle in the town, not a hill fortress (S11 Bytča).
- Bytča group: three women and one young man (Ficzkó). No interrogation or execution imagery; methods of execution stay out of VO and plates.
- Confinement: rooms in Csejte castle; no bricked-up cell, no slit window.
- Thurzó: use the 1607 Sadeler engraving (public domain) as the likeness reference.
- No sealed conspiracy document, no king signing, no bedside scene: none of these is in the record; all OUT.
- Portrait: the c.1630 oil portrait by an unknown artist, from the Nádasdy ancestors' gallery, now in the Hungarian National Museum collection (Commons record, refs mnm.hu); Commons files it under posthumous portraits. Painted after her death, so present it as "a portrait painted after her death". The HNM's own wording ("possibly an imagined likeness") is NOT in my captures: OPEN. Permitted VO if needed: "Her best known portrait was painted after her death." The painting's real inscription and arms must not be altered, extended or regenerated.

4. Execution imagery: an empty scaffold or empty courtyard at Bytča in grey winter light is an honest reconstruction (the sentences were carried out at once after the 7 Jan judgment, S3). Do NOT use a smoke column, pyre or fire glow: it signals the burning method and reads as spectacle. No blocks, axes or crowds.
