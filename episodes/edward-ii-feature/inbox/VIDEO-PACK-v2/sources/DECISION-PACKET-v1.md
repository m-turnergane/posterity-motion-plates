## (a) Brief
In March 1330 Edmund, Earl of Kent, was executed for trying to free his half-brother, Edward II, a king England had announced dead and buried at Gloucester more than two years earlier. The film asks **why a king's brother risked his life to restore a man already declared dead, and what the evidence can actually establish**. Kent is the human center. He landed with the 1326 invasion that cost Edward the crown, stood at the funeral, then believed the reports that Edward lived, and died for it.

**Viewer promise:** a real political thriller told from the records, with every claim weighed.

**Ending obligation:** separate what is established from what is only attributed, give the majority view fairly, and return to Kent.

**Out of scope:**
- a full biography of Edward
- the Italian tomb tradition
- the extended poker legend (one line, at most, saying the gruesome story came later)
- tactical battles
