## Why these six
DECISION-PACKET-v1 sec. (e) lists six Short heroes to regenerate or outpaint at 16:9 with the Short plate as reference: b01 funeral, b10 Corfe handover, b13 Nottingham, p14 Corfe dusk, b15 notary, b20 Kent seals. All are 941x1672 RGB (measured with PIL). As 16:9 crops they would need 2.04 output px per source px at 1920x1080, which the new `check_pixel_density` guardrail refuses (limit 1.5).

## Two routes per plate (owner picks one per plate)
- **OUTPAINT** keeps every original pixel at native size: a 2976x1674 canvas with the 941x1672 original centred, and only the sides painted. At 1920x1080 that is 0.645 px/px (0.684 at a 1.06 push), so it passes. The left and right of each 16:9 frame (about 68% of the width) are new painting.
- **REGENERATION** recomposes the shot for 16:9 with the Short plate attached as the hero/character reference. Target 2560x1440 or larger: 0.75 px/px static, 0.795 at 1.06. Under 1357x763 at a 1.06 push it is refused.
- House rules in every prompt: no on-image text of any kind, and no vignette, letterbox/pillarbox bars, borders or watermarks. The status phrases themselves (the IR tag, LEGEND/DOCUMENTED/RUMOUR) are deliberately **not** written into the prompts, because naming words in a negative prompt can make a generator paint them. Long-form changes from the Short prompts: 16:9 framing; no reserved caption lower third, title band or clear top tenth (the long-form picture is clean, with CC only and no chapter cards); push room ~106%; and sharp flames on every lit plate, so torch_v1c/fx_candle pass the fire-sharpness check (Laplacian >= 100 at native res).
- Save as the exact `expected_filename` below. The engine ingests by filename only.
