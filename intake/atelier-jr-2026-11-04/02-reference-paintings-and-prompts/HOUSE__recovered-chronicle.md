# Posterity — The Recovered Chronicle
**Status:** STANDING creative-production law (owner 2026-09-16)  
**Scope:** Strengthens authorship, visual consistency, story-to-image alignment, channel identity. Does **not** redesign brand, cadence, autonomy, or desk structure.

## Core principle
Posterity must feel **authored** even though faceless and agent-operated. Viewers should increasingly recognize a Posterity video without the channel name — via grammar, not a mechanical identical template. Civilizations/periods keep their own character.

**Conceptual frame:** The viewer has opened a recovered archive containing fragments of history that were forgotten, distorted, or misunderstood.  
**Internal name for the visual system:** **The Recovered Chronicle**.

## Narrator / editorial persona
House voice = **a calm archivist who distrusts neat stories, notices historical irony, cares what the surviving record supports, and is fascinated by the gap between what happened and what later generations remembered.**

Emerges through framing, sentence construction, observation, closing lines.  
Avoid: generic encyclopedia prose; gratuitous sarcasm; unsupported opinion; distorting evidence for drama.  
Always distinguish: documented history / scholarly inference / contested interpretation / legend / folklore / later tradition.

## Narrative (Scriptor)
Do not default to linear fact recitation. Where history supports it, use a dramatic engine (belief→contradiction→evidence→revision; ambition→obstacle→escalation→consequence; victory→hidden cost; legend→record→complication; mystery→explanations→strongest evidence). Revelation through story, not detached fact lists. Vary structure. Hooks = immediate narrative tension; no “Did you know?”

## Mandatory visual beat sheet (before editing)
Atelier must **not** improvise visuals from VO alone. Before full production, **Scriptor + Archivist** hand Atelier a beat sheet aligned to narration. Per major beat:
- timestamp / narration line
- story function
- specific person / object / place / action visible
- preferred asset class: surviving evidence | archival | map/diagram | historical artwork | reconstruction | typography | other
- historical constraints for accurate reconstruction
- source/reference when applicable
- emotional/narrative purpose of the shot

Every shot: evidence, clarity, geography/context, person/object, emotion, or narrative advance. Decorative filler = cut/replace.

## Three-layer visual grammar
1. **Evidence** — genuine surviving material when it strengthens the story (archaeology, museum objects, coins, statues, manuscripts, maps, inscriptions, period art, historical photos, contemporary illustrations). Archivist verifies provenance, relevance, commercial-use status. Not “old-looking” filler.
2. **Reconstruction** — synthetic when the record can’t supply the visual. Painterly, tactile, historically material (plates, illuminated chronicles, fresco, engraving, ink, gouache, weathered pigment, cinematic composition) — not glossy generic photoreal. Respect Archivist’s architecture/clothing/armour/objects/geography/material culture. No fantasy stereotypes.
3. **Editorial graphics** — maps, timelines, labels, inscriptions, quotes, diagrams, dates, typography when they communicate better than another illustration. Designed, not a slideshow of paintings.

## Brand DNA (persistent, not identical)
Tactile historical surfaces; restrained weathered colour; parchment/bone, charcoal/soot, bronze, deep muted foundations; episode-specific accents; chiaroscuro in reconstruction; strong silhouettes; intentional caption negative space; subtle archival texture; restrained, story-motivated motion (static/slow pushes core; controlled pans, punch-ins, map animation, parallax, match cuts, reveals OK when they strengthen a beat — no habitual micro-shake or gratuitous kinetic filler); maps/typography as storytelling.

**Art Bible:** `/workspace/posterity/brand/art-bible.md` — Vellum updates only when a new treatment materially improves the brand.

## Prohibited generic-AI shortcuts
Unless story-justified: generic fantasy armour; random hooded figures; unmotivated hero-camera stares; ubiquitous torches; floating magical particles; glowing dust filler; generic ruined temples; impossible monumental architecture; indiscriminate orange-teal grade; overly polished fantasy concept art; unmotivated burning cities; vague “ancient people”; attractive wrong-beat images. If a frame could belong to dozens of generic AI-history channels → replace candidate.

## Episode style-frame process
Before full synthetic set: Atelier creates ~3 hero/style frames (period interpretation, palette, lighting, character, material, composition, medium). **Vellum picks direction.** All subsequent synthetics inherit it. Prefer image/style/character references / ControlNet-class tools over prompt text alone. Seeds/wording alone ≠ continuity.

## Episode continuity
Recurring people/buildings/locations/objects → continuity record (age, appearance, clothing, armour, architecture, props, geography) unless narrative changes them.

## Post-production cohesion
Evidence + PD + reconstruction + graphics: consistent grade, captions, contrast, texture, motion language — without destroying integrity of genuine sources for forced uniformity.

## Sound-off test (mandatory before rough escalation)
Mute VO and check: broad story progression; beat match; no unexplained style jumps; recurring character/location coherence; no generic AI filler; archival feels intentional; random frames could read as same channel. Fix before human rough gate.

## Human review objective
Reduce human involvement. Daily rough review → publish gate, not art-direction session. Resolve routine visuals via this standard. Escalate: ambiguities, sensitive history, major format changes, spend, rights, strategy.

## Pulse experimentation
One primary experimental variable/hypothesis per upload when practical. Track chosen-to-view / stay, AVD, % viewed, retention shape, rewatches, likes, comments, shares, subs, follow-on. No single-upload overfit.

## Early Roman signal
Roman myth-correction has early traction — promising, not settled identity. Exploit enough for evidence; keep exploring forgotten kingdoms / archaeology / myth / folklore. Do not overfit to three uploads.


## Clarifications (LOCKED 2026-09-16 owner — minor)
1. **Motion is restrained, not necessarily slow.** Static holds and slow pushes remain core tools, but controlled pans, evidence punch-ins, map/diagram animation, parallax, match cuts, reveals and purposeful transitions are permitted when they clarify or strengthen a story beat. Avoid habitual micro-shake, fake handheld, gratuitous kinetic effects, or motion added solely because a still feels static.
2. **No standalone intro before the hook.** Day 1 sealed-archive language remains valid brand DNA, but a standalone branded intro must never delay the opening narrative hook. Prefer branding integrated into the first story beat, or only after attention is captured. Story first; branding through execution.
3. **System E is not a complete certainty taxonomy.** LEGEND/RUMOR vs DOCUMENTED is editorial emphasis, not a mandate to classify every statement into those buckets. Inferred / disputed / uncertain / later-tradition nuance → communicate accurately in narration/copy unless an explicit visual marker materially improves comprehension. Never convert “not legend” into “documented” by default.
4. **Controlled tests vs exploratory packages (Pulse).** Controlled test = reasonably isolated primary creative variable. Exploratory package = multiple meaningful variables differ → performance generates hypotheses, not causal proof for any one change. **Day 4 Zimbabwe = exploratory package** (topic-lane + visual-production changes simultaneous). Audience informs strategy; it does not solely define Posterity’s taste.

## Final standard
Never: “AI generated a script, found pictures, narrated, uploaded.”  
Always: “someone with taste, curiosity, and an extraordinary archive chose this story, investigated it, and decided exactly how it should be told.”  
**Automation = mechanism. Authorship = product.**
