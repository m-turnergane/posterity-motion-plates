## Verdicts and patches
1. "Newborn baby" at the vanishing: WRONG. The vanishing happened between 1587 and White's return (Virginia about three at the return), and it is known only from 1590. Patch: "a governor's daughter and her young daughter" (no age; "toddler" is true only at the 1590 date).
2. "Three years earlier, in 1587": MISLEADING. It dates the vanishing to 1590, which no source does (the year of the vanishing is unknown), and it spends the Ch II return. Patch: "In 1587, Governor John White, her father, had brought them..." which also ties the governor to the daughter (weak link in the original: "a governor's daughter" is only resolved in line 8).
3. "The old English outpost was abandoned. Its soldiers were missing." Outpost abandoned: DOCUMENTED (White: fort razed, houses standing, overgrown, deer in them). The earlier garrison: Lane's 1585-86 colony left in 1586; Grenville then left fifteen men; White found only bones of one man and no sign of the other fifteen (vj-white-1587 l.268-292). "Soldiers" is loose (Grenville's fifteen were a holding party). Patch: "The fifteen men left to guard it were gone." (a first-time viewer also gets who). Do NOT say killed (White blames the Native men, unproven for all fifteen).
4. "Near the reeds" and crabs: PARTLY. Howe was wading alone in the water, catching crabs with a forked stick, two miles from his company (documented, 28 July). The reeds are where the attackers hid. Patch: "killed while gathering crabs, alone in the water." Crabbing: DOCUMENTED.
5. "Only then did he agree": OVERCLAIM on order. The 25 Aug statement itself says he had "at last, though much against his will... yielded", and he left 27 Aug. The statement records the yielding, it does not trigger it. Patch: "They signed a testimony saying the request was theirs. Against his will, he agreed." (DOCUMENTED).
6. "First English child born in North America": NPS wording is "first child of English parentage" in the Americas, and White's own words are "first Christian borne in Virginia". Patch: "in the Americas" (as in the live script). "North America" is defensible but not the source's wording.
7. "Deserting": QUALIFIED. White's own words are about "discredit" and enemies saying he led them there to leave them behind. "Deserting" is a fair paraphrase; "abandoning" is closer. Either passes; I kept "deserting" in the patched version because it is the owner's word.
8. Tagline last: PASS.

Other weak links (patch suggestions): (a) "But their captain put them ashore on Roanoke instead" gives no reason, so a first-time viewer cannot tell it was the captain's choice. Patch: "But their captain said summer was nearly gone and put them ashore on Roanoke instead." (+6w, DOCUMENTED, partisan but stated as his reason). (b) The hook says "vanished" with no hint that White found it so, which is fine for Ch II's payoff (nothing about the post, the word or the cross is spent). (c) "Soon kept English ships near home" is documented (the Queen's order), though "war with Spain" arrives without the fleet detail; fine for runtime. (d) Croatoan and Manteo are not named, so Ch II still owns them.
