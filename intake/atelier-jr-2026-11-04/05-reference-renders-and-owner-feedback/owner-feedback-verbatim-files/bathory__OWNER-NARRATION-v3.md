# Elizabeth Báthory — The Blood Countess
### Posterity · Halloween v3 · Owner-proposed narration (received 2026-10-31 3:27pm ET) — PRIMARY EDITORIAL BASIS

Elizabeth Báthory was accused of burning servants with hot irons. Of forcing others into freezing winter water.

People said young women were dying in her household. The rumours reached the king.

He sent Hungary's highest official, György Thurzó, to investigate.

One December night, armed men arrived at her manor. Báthory was eating supper.

Thurzó later claimed his men found one girl dead and another gravely wounded.

Báthory was taken to the fortress above her home.

Four attendants were arrested as her alleged accomplices. Interrogated under torture, they confessed. Three were executed within days.

But Báthory was never tried.

The king pressed for a trial. Thurzó prevented it. Her powerful relatives feared disgrace, while many witnesses could only repeat what they'd heard.

She died in confinement.

Then someone added the detail that would make her infamous.

More than a century after her death, a priest wrote that she bathed in murdered girls' blood to stay young.

No witness had described that.

No court ruled on her guilt.

But the legend had its monster: the Blood Countess.

History remembers the victors. We remember the rest.
