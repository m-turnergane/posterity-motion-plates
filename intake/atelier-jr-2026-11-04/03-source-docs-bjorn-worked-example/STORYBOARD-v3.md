# STORYBOARD v3 (OWNER-APPROVED, VO v3 LOCKED): Björn Ironside, "The King Who Sacked the Wrong Rome"

## OWNER SUMMARY (read this first)
1. **Spine:** a dead man is carried into a church with a sword hidden in his coffin. Legend says Björn Ironside led a fleet to conquer Rome, and his old tutor Hastein planned to crown him emperor there.
2. The heist plays straight: a city by the sea that looks like Rome, barred gates, a fake deathbed conversion, mercy from the bishop and the count, a baptism, a funeral, and the "corpse" leaping out.
3. **Reveal 1 (68.5s): "This was not Rome."** The same city we saw gleaming at dusk is shown in plain daylight: a small walled cathedral town with wooden houses, Roman ruins and a lagoon. It is Luna.
4. **Reveal 2 (74.3 to 88.2s): the sources.** The first known teller, Dudo, names only Hastein. Björn appears in the story only decades later, in William of Jumièges. On screen, one shadow on Dudo's wall becomes two on William's.
5. Payoff: Björn, half-painted and half-sketch, on the wall of the wrong city. Then Luna's ruins at first light, with the tagline spoken.
6. **Atlas: RUNS (owner decision).** P05 plays at 17.5 to 23.75s. The desk supplies the verified map reference (in progress), the owner paints a clean atlas against it, and Atelier animates the routes on top: solid ink for the documented AB route, a broken lighter line for the legendary leg, and an unlabelled coastal end-mark. Luna is never labelled.
7. **Björn's absence: approved.** He is off screen from 17.5s to 80.5s (about 63s), so his late entry at William's desk *is* the second reveal.
8. **Runtime 97.95s (about 98s). 20 plates plus a unique outro (21 entries).** No added holds or plates. No plate is over 7s, there is no readable text anywhere, and no gore.
