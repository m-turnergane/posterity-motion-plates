## NARRATION
```
L01 The legends say Björn Ironside sailed to conquer Rome, and sacked the wrong city.
L02 But who was really leading his fleet?
L03 Later writers made him the legendary Ragnar Lothbrok's son, and said no weapon could wound him.
L04 The legend says his tutor Hastein, a veteran raider, planned to crown him emperor in Rome.
L05 A Viking fleet really did slip between Spain and Africa, and raid southern France and Italy.
L06 A monk wrote that a storm drove them to Luna, an old Roman city in Italy.
L07 Its people barred the gates. Hastein believed it was Rome.
L08 He sent word that he was dying, and wished to be baptised.
L09 That night, his men announced his death.
L10 They carried his coffin into the church, armour hidden under their tunics.
L11 Then Hastein leapt out, sword in hand, and cut down the bishop.
L12 Only then did they learn it was not Rome.
L13 But the Frankish annals written at the time never name the fleet's leader.
L14 They name one Viking chief called Björn, who swore loyalty to the Frankish king.
L15 Then he vanishes from the record, before the fleet ever sailed.
L16 His place aboard comes from that monk, writing two centuries later.
L17 So who led the fleet that sacked the wrong Rome? No record from his lifetime puts Björn aboard.
TAG History remembers the victors. We remember the rest.
```

## CHARACTER CONTINUITY
- **Björn (legend):** early twenties, tall and broad, dark-blond hair braided on top with shaved sides, short braided beard, kohl-darkened eyes, leather jerkin, wolf-fur mantle, iron arm rings, plain round shield. Original face, never an actor likeness. Anchor: P01.
- **Hastein:** fifties, lean and weathered, greying braid at the nape, close grey beard, narrow watchful eyes, deep-hooded dark wool cloak with a fur collar. Clearly a different face from Björn. Anchor: P04.
- **Bishop of Luna:** elderly, tonsured, white vestments with a plain chasuble, crozier and gospel book, no mitre (ninth century).
- **Count of Luna:** middle-aged Italian noble, belted tunic, cloak pinned with a brooch.
- **Charles the Bald:** Carolingian king, simple gold crown, short beard, rich mantle.
- **Berno:** a Viking chief, thirties, different face from Björn, kneeling in a fur cloak.
- **The monk (William of Jumièges):** Benedictine, black habit, tonsure, eleventh-century Norman abbey.
